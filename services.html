Our Services

There are many ways to be part of our work. Whether you are looking to adopt, foster, or volunteer, here is everything you need to know to get started.

Adoption Process

Adopting a pet is a lifelong commitment. Our adoption process is designed to make sure every animal goes to a home that is the right fit for them and for you.

  1. Browse: Look through our available animals and find a pet that suits your lifestyle.
  2. Apply: Complete an adoption application so we can learn about you and your home.
  3. Meet and Greet: Spend time with the animal to see if you are a good match.
  4. Home Check: A member of our team visits to make sure your home is safe for your new pet.
  5. Adoption Day: Sign the adoption agreement, pay the adoption fee, and take your new family member home.

All of our animals are vaccinated, microchipped, and spayed or neutered before adoption.

Foster Program

Foster homes give animals a safe and loving place to stay while they recover, grow, or wait for adoption. Fostering frees up space in our shelter so that we can rescue more animals.

What We Provide

  • Supplies: Food, bedding, toys, and any other supplies your foster animal needs.
  • Medical Care: All veterinary care is covered by Twin Cities Animal Rescue.
  • Support: A foster coordinator is available to answer questions and give guidance.

What We Ask of You

  • Time: Care for your foster animal for anything from a few weeks to a few months.
  • Love: Give the animal attention, patience, and a calm place to settle in.
  • Updates: Share photos and notes so we can help your foster animal find the right home.

Volunteer Roles

Our volunteers are the heart of our organization. Choose a role that matches your skills and the time you have available.

  • Animal Care Assistant: Feed, clean, and exercise the animals in our shelter.
  • Dog Walker: Take our dogs on daily walks to keep them happy and healthy.
  • Adoption Event Helper: Help set up and run adoption events and introduce animals to potential adopters.
  • Transport Driver: Drive animals to vet appointments, foster homes, and adoption events.
  • Fundraising and Outreach: Help plan fundraisers and spread the word about our work in the community.
  • Photographer: Take photos of our animals to help them find homes faster.
