Our Service Area

We rescue and rehome animals across the twin cities of Kitwe and Ndola and the surrounding Copperbelt Province of Zambia, including:

  • Kitwe
  • Ndola
  • Chingola
  • Mufulira
  • Luanshya
  • Chililabombwe

If you live outside these areas, please still get in touch and we will do our best to help or refer you to a partner organization.
