About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue was founded in 2010 by a group of passionate animal lovers who wanted to make a difference in the lives of abandoned and abused animals. Over the years, we have grown into a dedicated team of volunteers and staff committed to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming animals in need.

Our Mission

Our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in need. We are committed to promoting animal welfare and providing a safe haven for abandoned and abused animals.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion: We treat all animals with kindness and empathy.
  • Integrity: We operate with honesty and transparency in all our actions.
  • Community: We believe in the power of community to make a difference in the lives of animals.
  • Advocacy: We advocate for animal welfare and work to raise awareness about the importance of responsible pet ownership.

Our Partners

We work closely with local shelters, veterinary clinics, and other animal welfare organizations to ensure that we can provide the best care for the animals we rescue. Our partners help us with medical care, foster homes, and adoption events.
